On April 17th, InfoComm China 2024 was held in Beijing, during which Saphlux presented several display products: the MG Series QD-mLED COB All-In-One Display developed in collaboration with Leyard, the P0.9 QD-mLED COB Display Module developed in collaboration with Lopu, and the self-developed mLED microdisplay product for AR devices. Concurrently, the 2024 Leyard Eco-Partner Conference took place in Beijing. The official debut of the Direct View QD-mLED Display products at the conference attracted widespread attention from the media in the industry.
