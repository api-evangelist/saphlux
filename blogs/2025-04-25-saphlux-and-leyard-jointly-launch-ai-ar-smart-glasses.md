Saphlux and Leyard have jointly unveiled their co-developed AI+AR smart glasses project at the 2025 Leyard Strategic Partner Conference. Integrating cutting-edge technologies such as Micro-LED displays, AI assistants, and intelligent interaction features, the product is among the first AI-enhanced AR glasses to reach mass production. It is designed to deliver immersive, visualized information experiences in scenarios such as conferences and tourism.
