From April 8 to 10, 2026, Touch Taiwan 2026—the International Smart Display and Touch Panel Exhibition—was held at the Nangang Exhibition Center, Hall 1, in Taipei, China. Dr. Chen Chen, CEO of Saphlux, was invited to attend and delivered a keynote address at the Core Technology Forum organized by the Taiwan Semiconductor and Optoelectronic Materials and Devices Industry Association (TSOMDA).
