On March 7, 2025, Saphlux, Inc. officially unveiled the QD-COB Pro Series, a professional-level quantum dot Micro-LED direct-view display solution, at the Shenzhen International Smart Display & System Integration Exhibition (ISLE). QD-COB Pro is the first Micro-LED direct-view display product to achieve full-angle color accuracy with no color shift.
