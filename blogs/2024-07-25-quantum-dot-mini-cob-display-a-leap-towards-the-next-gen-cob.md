QD-mLED direct-view display technology has been steadily progressing towards the goal of "achieving ultimate display technology" since it was first jointly exhibited by Saphlux and Leyard in April 2023. This year, Saphlux has officially initiated the commercialization of its QD-mLED COB Display Product. Within a few months of its market debut, QD-mLED COB displays have quickly gained market acceptance, with nearly a thousand square meters of products already deployed.
