On March 23rd, CCTV's "Network News Broadcast" aired a series of reports titled "Frontline Research: Accelerating the Formation of New Productivity," highlighting Saphlux’s research and development of quantum dot technology and its QD-mLED product. Earlier, CCTV's Chinese International Channel also featured a special report on this technology under the theme "Pursuing Light - Wings of Intelligence." Saphlux's Director of Technology, Hengyang Sun, was interviewed by CCTV. Saphlux, Inc.
