On March 2, 2026, the world’s first industry group standard for photoluminescent quantum dot Mini/MicroLED displays—Technical Specification for Photoluminescent Quantum Dot Mini/Micro LED Display Terminals (T/SUCA 053-2026)—was officially released and will take effect on March 12, 2026. The standard was proposed and administered by the Shenzhen 8K UHD Video Industry Cooperation Alliance . Drafting organizations include Leyard Optoelectronic Co., Ltd.
