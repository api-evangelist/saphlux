On November 5th, 2025, the 8th China International Import Expo (CIIE) was held in Shanghai. Saphlux was invited to exhibit in the Innovation Zone, presenting its core display technologies and terminal-ready solutions. During the event, the company not only unveiled multiple cutting-edge advancements but was also named one of Haichuanghui’s “Top 10 Projects,” in recognition of its technological leadership.
