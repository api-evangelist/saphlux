SHENZHEN, CHINA & ROCHESTER, NY, September xx, 2025 – Saphlux, Inc. (“Saphlux”), a leader in quantum dot MicroLED displays, and Vuzix® Corporation (NASDAQ: VUZI ) ("Vuzix"), a leading supplier of AI-powered smart glasses, waveguides and Augmented Reality (AR) technologies, today announced a strategic collaboration to deliver a high-performance optical solution, accelerating the adoption of next-generation AI/AR glasses. The Saphlux–Vuzix collaboration will enable a new compact optical component design for next-generation AI/AR glasses.
