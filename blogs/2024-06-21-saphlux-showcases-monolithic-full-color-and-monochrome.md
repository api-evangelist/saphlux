From June 18 to 20, the Augmented World Expo 2024 (AWE2024) took place at the Long Beach Convention & Entertainment Center in California, USA. AWE is one of the most influential events in the global augmented and virtual reality technology field. It serves not only as a platform to showcase the latest technological innovations and products but also provides attendees with ample opportunities for networking and collaboration.
