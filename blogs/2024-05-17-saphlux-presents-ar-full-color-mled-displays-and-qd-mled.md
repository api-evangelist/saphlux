The SID Display Week 2024 was grandly held from May 14 to 16 at the San Jose Convention Center in California, USA. As the most influential professional event in the global display industry, SID brings together professionals and innovators from around the world to showcase the latest technologies, products, and applications. Saphlux, as a leader in QD mLED display solutions, participated in the exhibition with its QD-mLED Direct View display modules and AR full-color and monochrome mLED microdisplays featuring the proprietary NPQD® technology, attracting widespread attention from attendees.
