June 25, 2026 – Saphlux, a global leader in silicon-based µLED microdisplay technology, and Ennostar Corporation, a leading vertically integrated optoelectronic semiconductor company , have officially entered into a strategic partnership to advance the commercialization of semi-polar Micro-LED technology. The collaboration will focus on two key areas: AR microdisplays and high-speed optical communications. This partnership brings together the complementary strengths of both companies.
