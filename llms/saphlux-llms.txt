# Saphlux

> Saphlux is a quantum dot Micro-LED (QD-MLED) display technology company founded in 2014 out of
> Yale University, headquartered in San Diego, California, with operations in China and Japan. It
> commercialized semi-polar (20-21) GaN-on-sapphire epitaxial wafers and now mass-produces NPQD
> quantum-dot Micro-LED devices for large-format commercial displays and wearable AR micro-displays.

## Products

- [AR Micro-LED Display](https://www.saphlux.com/): T-Series monolithic full-color Micro-LED
  micro-displays for all-day wearable AR devices and AI smart glasses.
- [Quantum Dot COB Display](https://www.saphlux.com/): QD-COB and QD-COB Pro Series chip-on-board
  panels for >100-inch commercial and home-theater displays.
- [Technology](https://www.saphlux.com/technology): semi-polar (20-21) GaN on sapphire, NPQD
  nanopore quantum-dot color conversion, monolithic full-color integration.
